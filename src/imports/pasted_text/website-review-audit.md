# Consolidated Website Review & UX Audit

After reviewing all the feedback collected from different users, several common observations appeared repeatedly. These recurring points indicate the areas that would have the greatest impact on improving the overall user experience, credibility, and conversion rate of the website.

---

# Common Feedback (Repeated by Multiple Reviewers)

## 1. Website Length & Navigation

One of the most common observations is that the homepage feels quite long. Users need to continuously scroll through multiple chapters, which may become repetitive and cause some visitors to lose interest before reaching the final sections.

Several reviewers also mentioned that when they revisit the website to read a specific section, they have to scroll through the page again to locate it, making navigation less convenient.

The chapter-based navigation on the right side was appreciated by almost everyone because it provides a unique storytelling experience. However, the chapter names are currently visible only when users hover over the indicators.

### Recommendation

* Keep the chapter names permanently visible instead of showing them only on hover.
* Allow users to quickly identify and jump directly to any chapter.
* This will reduce unnecessary scrolling and significantly improve navigation and usability.
* Consider shortening certain sections where possible without affecting the overall story flow.

---

## 2. Clearer User Journey

Another common observation is that the website primarily speaks to businesses, while investors receive comparatively less information.

Several reviewers mentioned that if they landed on the website as investors, they would understand Karncy's business offerings but would not clearly understand:

* How investing works
* The investor onboarding journey
* Investment opportunities
* Investor benefits
* Why investors should choose Karncy?

### Recommendation

Create two clearly defined user journeys:

* Business Journey
* Investor Journey

Ensure both audiences immediately understand where they belong and what action they should take.

---

## 3. Stronger Call-to-Action (CTA)

Many users felt that important CTAs are not prominent enough throughout the website.

Examples include:

* Apply for Funding
* Become an Investor
* Talk to Karncy
* Get Started
* Sign Up

Some CTA buttons also have low visual prominence, making them easy to overlook.

### Recommendation

* Increase CTA visibility.
* Use stronger button styling.
* Improve color contrast.
* Increase font weight.
* Place important CTAs at logical conversion points throughout the website.
* Add a clear Sign Up or Get Started CTA in the footer.

---

## 4. Build More Trust & Credibility

Trust was another frequently mentioned topic.

Several reviewers suggested that users investing money or doing business expect to see stronger trust indicators before engaging with the platform.

### Recommendation

Display:

* ISO 27001 Certification
* CERT-In Certification
* SOC Report (if available)
* Company Registration Details
* CIN / Registration Number
* Office Address
* Official Business Email
* Contact Number
* LinkedIn Profile

Also showcase:

* Strong Governance Framework
* Compliance Practices
* Risk Management
* Transparent Operations

Adding these elements will significantly improve confidence among investors and businesses.

---

## 5. Homepage Storytelling

Many reviewers appreciated the storytelling concept and agreed that it makes Karncy different from traditional fintech websites.

However, several users suggested making the story even stronger.

Instead of introducing products immediately, the homepage should begin with a larger vision.

Rather than positioning Karncy as another fintech platform, the story should gradually explain:

* The future of commerce
* Evolution of value exchange
* Ownership in the digital economy
* Programmable commerce
* Why today's financial systems need transformation

Only after building this narrative should Karncy introduce itself as the solution.

This approach creates a stronger emotional connection and positions Karncy as a category creator rather than simply another finance company.

---

## 6. Homepage Should Feel More Dynamic

Several users felt that after a few sections, the page begins to feel repetitive.

Although the design is visually premium, adding more interactions would make browsing more enjoyable.

### Recommendation

Introduce:

* Hover animations
* Scroll animations
* Entrance animations
* Micro interactions
* Button hover effects
* Card hover effects
* Smooth transitions

These interactions will make the experience feel more modern and engaging.

---

## 7. Stronger Social Proof

Trust-building content was another recurring suggestion.

### Recommendation

Add:

* Customer Success Stories
* Business Case Studies
* Client Logos
* Partner Logos
* Technology Partner Logos

A continuously scrolling logo section will immediately improve credibility.

---

## 8. Homepage Statistics

Multiple reviewers recommended showcasing Karncy's business scale more prominently.

### Recommendation

Display animated counters for:

* Total Funding Facilitated
* Businesses Supported
* Active Investors
* Cities Covered
* Transactions Completed
* Average Approval Time

Animated counters immediately communicate business maturity and success.

---

## 9. Why Choose Karncy Section

Several reviewers suggested creating a dedicated section explaining why customers should choose Karncy.

### Suggested Points

* Technology-driven platform
* Faster approvals
* Transparent process

* Robust risk management

## 10. Navigation Bar

Reviewers also suggested introducing a more conventional navigation bar to complement the storytelling approach.

### Recommendation

# Additional UX Review & Heuristic Evaluation

## 1. Article Cards

The article cards do not have thumbnail images, making this section look plain and less engaging compared to standard blog layouts.

### Recommendation

Add relevant, high-quality thumbnail images for each article card to provide visual context and encourage users to click.

---

## 2. Missing Primary CTA

This section does not have a clear primary Call-to-Action (CTA) button, which may leave users unsure about what to do next.

### Recommendation

Add a high-contrast primary CTA button below the main content to give users a clear next step, such as exploring more or getting started.

## 4. Solution Cards

Roman numerals are currently used to represent the three solution cards.

### Recommendation

Replace the Roman numerals with meaningful icons or thumbnail images so users can recognize each category more easily.

---

## 5. "ENTER" CTA

The **"ENTER"** links are not very noticeable because of their small font size and low-contrast color.

### Recommendation

Increase the font size and weight, convert them into proper buttons, and consider changing the text to:

* Step In
* Dive In
* Explore
* Learn More

## 6. Trust & Security

The website does not currently display visible trust seals or security certifications.

### Recommendation

Add certifications such as:

* ISO 27001
* CERT-In
* SOC Report (if available)

Display them near the value proposition section.

---

## 7. Solutions Section

If Solution I, II, and III remain as dedicated sections instead of popups, the **"Open Deep Dive"** CTA needs stronger emphasis.

The current images also do not appear relevant.

### Recommendation

* Style the CTA as a button.
* Replace the current visuals with more meaningful images related to each solution.

---

## 8. AI-like Formatting

Some content contains AI-style punctuation (—), making the copy feel machine-generated.

### Recommendation

Remove unnecessary long hyphens and use natural sentence formatting.

---

## 9. Underlined Text

Currently, clicking underlined text opens a separate page.

### Recommendation

Show the information in a popup/modal instead of redirecting users to another page.

---

## 10. Chapter 07 – Step 6

Replace **"Returns"** with **"Returns (ROI)"** for better clarity.

---

## 11. Footer CTA

The footer currently lacks a clear Sign Up or Get Started CTA.

### Recommendation

Add a prominent CTA in the footer.

---

## 12. Background Design

The page background appears quite simple.

### Recommendation

Use a subtle seamless pattern with low opacity, or apply decorative background elements to the left and right sides for more visual depth.

---

## 13. How It Works Section

The current 2×3 grid layout does not clearly communicate a step-by-step workflow.

### Recommendation

* Add arrows or connecting lines between the steps.
* Increase the font size of the **"Steps"** heading.
* Use a semi-bold font weight.

## 14. Small Labels

Labels such as **"BUSINESSES SUPPORTED"** use very small uppercase text with a light font weight.

### Recommendation

Increase the font size, increase the font weight, and use darker colors for better readability and WCAG compliance.

---

## 15. Testimonials

The testimonial labels are too small.

### Recommendation

Increase their size and include either:

* Company logos
* Professional reviewer headshots

to improve authenticity and trust.

---

## 16. Paragraph Width

Some paragraphs do not fully utilize the available content width, resulting in inconsistent layouts.

### Recommendation

Adjust paragraph widths for consistent alignment and improved readability.

---

## 17. Article Cards

The article cards again lack thumbnail images.

### Recommendation

Add relevant, high-quality thumbnails to improve engagement and click-through rates.

## 18. Chapter 10 Alignment

Align it to the left for consistency.

Add a subtle text shadow or slightly darken the background behind the text.

## 21. Replay Button

The text inside the **Replay** button has low contrast.

Increase text contrast for better readability.

* Increase the length of shorter descriptions where appropriate.

* Slightly increase the logo size.

---

## 25. Product Labels

The labels for the three products are relatively small.

### Recommendation

Increase the font size to improve visibility.

---

## 26. Remove "Scroll" Text

The **"Scroll"** text displayed in Chapter 0 feels unnecessary.

### Recommendation

Remove it to create a cleaner hero section.

---

## 27. Floating Action Button

The text inside the rounded floating button at the bottom-right is difficult to read because of the dark background.

### Recommendation

Improve text contrast and visibility by adjusting the button color, background, or typography.

---

## 28. Deep Dive Pages

On the Solution I, Solution II, and Solution III deep dive pages, the **"Back to the Journey"** button appears at both the top and bottom of the page.

### Recommendation

Displaying both buttons feels repetitive. A cleaner approach would be to:

* Keep only the top button and remove the bottom one, **or**
* Hide the top button after scrolling and display the bottom button when users reach the end.

This provides the same functionality while keeping the interface cleaner.

---

# Overall Feedback

The website has a modern, premium, and visually appealing design with a unique storytelling approach that differentiates Karncy from traditional corporate websites. The chapter-based navigation creates an engaging experience, and the platform's key offerings—including Invoice Financing, Joint Ventures, and Startup Partnerships—are presented clearly. Sections such as **How It Works**, statistics, testimonials, FAQs, and business information contribute positively to user trust.

The primary opportunity for improvement lies in strengthening navigation, reducing repetitive scrolling, creating distinct journeys for businesses and investors, increasing the visibility of important CTAs, enhancing trust through compliance and social proof, and introducing richer interactions and animations. Addressing these areas will make the website feel more intuitive, engaging, and conversion-focused while reinforcing Karncy's positioning as a premium platform.
